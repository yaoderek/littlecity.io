Market day in Little City

How to get it: Lay a plaza at least three by three with two stalls on it, and houses round it. On market mornings traders set up stalls there, a busker juggles and the town comes to buy.

Market day is one of the things a town does by itself in Little City, a hand-drawn town diorama builder. Nobody schedules it: build the right places and it happens on its own.
