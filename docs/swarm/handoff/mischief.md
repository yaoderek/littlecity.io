# Mischief handoff (branch `swarm/mischief`)

Design section 13b. Lightning (tool id `torch`), Bomb, Nuke, Mallet, Mend; ruins; the town fighting fires and mending itself. The raid is not started.

## Try it
- `npm run dev`, `/?seed=4242&mischief`: a grown town, the Mischief drawer open. Undo calls off anything going on. Menu: Rebuild on/off.
- Galleries: `npm run shots -- mischief-ruins-houses` / `mischief-ruins-kinds`; sequences on `mischief-street`, e.g. `node src/life/testing/sequence.mjs mischief-street --edit 'fire@18,1,21' --warm 3000 --frames 8 --every 3500 --zoom 10 --target 19.5,1,22.5 --yaw 20 --pitch 40` (the pump cart), `--edit 'nuke@20,1,20' --delay 3300 --frames 20 --every 500 --zoom 1.3 --target 20,4,20` (the nuke; it goes off ~3.45 s after the drop). Verbs: fire, strike, bomb, nuke, knock, mend, mendall, undo.

## How it is built
- Saved: `Block.ruin` (`src/world/damage.ts`): stage, cause, mending + 32 steps of progress (or, not mending, which way a tree fell: `ruinFall`), settling (never saved); edits `setRuin`, `repair`; codec v4 section bit 1. A nuke's crater is ordinary `land` edits in the same act.
- Drawn: `src/gen/damage/` cuts each generator's own mesh (ruins, solid debris parts, mending phases, scaffolding); things on stilts (a dock, or anything standing on one: `afloat`) go over whole into the water at stage 3 (blast) and lean at stage 2. Big ships: `harbour.strike` (ships/visit.ts) marks them scorched/dismasted (they leave) or broken (`sinking`, under the water, berth freed); `shipwreck.system.ts` draws torn sails, the fallen mast, fire, smoke, wreckage, and the crew of a ship going down swimming for open water and the map's edge; barges and passing ships too; small boats through `boatwreck.ts` (each boat's pose calls `boatPose`); swimmers with a shore in reach are handed to the crowd (`crowd.ashore`) and walk in at a door. Lightning can strike a vessel (`director.vesselAt`, `strikeVessel`). A fire on a building being mended keeps it mending (lifeless). Sequences: `--ship moored|underway` waits for a ship and aims at it, then `shipbomb@<d>,0,0` / `shipnuke@<d>,0,0` hit it from d cells off its sea side.
- Engine `src/life/mischief/engine.ts` (fire, blast with `fly`/`rim` options, `blastPlan`/`hit` for the nuke, knock, repair, douse, `sinceHit`).
- `director.ts`: acts = undo steps; mending is a property of the damage (`mendTick`: every damaged, unburning building starts mending `MEND.quiet` + a seeded stagger after it was last hurt, a step every `MEND.step` s, faster with a crew; amends, no undo step of its own); the nuke (`NUKE`, crater; its hits are done in 0.2 s batches as the shockwave ring reaches them, clock in `nuke-timing.ts`); camera yaw for flames.
- Live drawing: `mischief.system.ts` (flames, fused smoke and dust, sparks, firelight, embers, bomb, flash, chunk flights, fallen sails), `lightning.system.ts` (storm cloud gathering, bolt at `STRIKE.at` 0.6 s when `director.strike` lights the block, flash, cold light, cloud thinning by 4.2 s), `nuke.system.ts` (casing, flash core, shockwave ring/band/dust-wall arcs, shudders, fireball → stem + rolling cap → smoke → wash, firelight, ash, scorched crater, embers), `brigade.system.ts` (pump cart, laid hoses, helmets, continuous jet, bucket slosh, steam), `sound.system.ts` (crackle, hiss, bell, siren, whistle, bangs). Every handle they draw goes through `owned.ts`, so life's dispose takes all of it back (a new town keeps no trace); director events `reset` (called off) and `undo` drop what is in the air; `director.drawn()` counts what is showing (browser tests).
- People `response.ts` (chains in the living town, listed first in `src/life/town/index.ts`): fire (the pump cart kept by the main well or fountain: muster, wheel, set down, suction hose in, hose laid behind the nozzle man, pump, water; wound in and wheeled home; bucket chains for fires the cart is not at; clear out, onlookers, glum residents; bombs and the nuke: run, creep back) and rebuild (crews of two visit up to four mending buildings by day, standing beside it, on a deck, or on the shore within 4 cells; with no footing it mends at a crew's pace anyway, `director.remote`). Scorch fades: a building's ring with its mending, the nuke's from 30 to 210 s (`SCORCH`, session only, not saved).

## Found and fixed (owner: "nothing mended on its own")
A bomb lights timber nearby; the old rebuild waited for every fire in town to go out (60 s and more) and then took one building at a time. Test: `src/app/mischief.test.ts` "a wrecked building starts mending on its own even while a fire burns elsewhere".
