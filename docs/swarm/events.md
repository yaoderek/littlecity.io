# Everything the town does by itself

One line each: what happens, where, when, how often, and its rule in the
catalogue (part L). The guide (`src/guide/entries.ts`) has an entry with a
picture for each one marked **guide**, in the group named. Things that are
always on (smoke, birds, flags, boats bobbing) are listed at the end.

## Big days
- **A wedding** (guide) — a chapel whose door is on a path within 20 steps of a plaza (or a street), houses round about; by day, the first 25–45 s after the town opens, then every 300–420 s; up to 20 guests from within 32 steps. L57. Note with Watch.
- **A raid** (guide) — by longship onto a beach, else rowing boats onto any low shore; on its own only in a town of 14 buildings or more with a shore a ship can reach, by day, after 8 minutes of play then every 12–20 minutes (only while the menu's Raiders switch is on, off by default); or any time with the Raid tool. Sink the ship (bomb, lightning) before it lands and nobody comes ashore. L69–L72. Note with Watch.
- **Market day** (guide) — the biggest square (a full 3 × 3 plaza) with two stalls or more on or beside it; market days (the first day, then 60 % of days) from dawn to early afternoon. L56.
- **The midwinter festival** (guide) — every town: lights along streets and round squares, wreaths, a tree on the main square; the last third of winter, going up house by house, lit from dusk. D35 (seasons).

## Through the seasons
- **A beach day** (guide) — a town of 8 houses or more with 4+ sand cells at the water within 40 steps of a front door; summer, fair weather, by day; 15–35 s after the town opens, then 60–120 s after the last ends; the ice-cream cart comes too. L63.
- **Picnics** (guide) — open grass with nothing but grass or garden all round, 2–30 steps from a front door, trees nearby first; spring (summer too in a town with no beach), fair, by day; every 2–4 minutes. L66.
- **The harvest** (guide) — a wheat field with a windmill that can be walked to; late summer and autumn, by day, dry. L59.
- **A harvest supper** (guide) — the biggest square (a full 3 × 3 plaza); autumn, from 110 s before the end of the day into dusk, once a day (every 5–7 minutes with the time held). L65.
- **A snow day** (guide) — the biggest square, else the longest street; once snow lies deep, by day; then every 2.5–4 minutes. L64.
- **Carol singers** (guide) — a town of 6 houses or more; winter or the festival, at dusk, once an evening (every 3–5 minutes with the time held at dusk or night). L67.

## By the water
- **Ships unloading and carters** (guide) — a quay with shops, an inn or stalls within 24 steps; whenever a galleon, cog or galley is in, by day. L38–L40, L58.
- **Waving a ship in** (guide) — a pier or quay where ships call; when a big ship comes in by day. L68.
- **The catch comes in** (guide) — a pier with a fish stall or fish shop within 20 steps; dawn and by day, every half minute or so. L53.
- River barges (L41), the fishing boat out at dawn and home at dusk (L18), the rower (L20), passing sails (L42): ambience, not in the guide.

## Every day
- **Bread from the mill** (guide) — a windmill and a bakery (else the inn) the miller can walk between; dawn and by day. L51.
- **Fetching water** (guide) — a well or a fountain near houses; by day, now and then. L52.
- **Washing day** (guide) — a laundry line between houses; out in the morning, in at dusk or in rain. L60.
- **School and hopscotch** (guide) — a schoolhouse with families near; a lesson every 150 s by day. L62.
- **The smith at work** (guide) — a smithy; by day. L62.
- **A show** (guide) — a bandstand or a theatre; a show of 60 s every 210 s, by day and dusk. L62.
- **Games on the field** (guide) — a playing field (its shape picks the game); by day and into dusk, every minute or so after the last. Field chain.
- **Dogs** (guide) — a town of 6 houses or more: every sixth house keeps one. L61.
- **A scuffle** (guide) — any town with people in the streets; now and then, then a rest of 75–130 s. L50.
- The garrison's patrol and changing of the guard, the rider: in the guide already as the Garrison and Rider entries (a castle). L36, L37.
- Builders and onlookers when you place something big (L54): not in the guide (it needs the player to place something while the picture is taken).
- Maypole dancing (village green), apple picking (orchard), wall-booth vendors: in the guide under their places (Village green, Orchard, Wall shops). L62.

## Evening and night
- **The lamplighter** (guide) — street lamps along the paths; at dusk. L08, L23.
- **The night watch** (guide) — a town of 12 houses or more and no castle; at night. L55.
- Windows lighting as people come home (L07), the lighthouse beam (L09), stargazers and night fishers (L25): ambience.

## After mischief
- **The fire brigade** (guide) — a fire anywhere in a town with a well or a fountain: the pump cart, hoses, a bucket chain; then crews mend the burnt buildings under scaffolding. Mischief (design 13b).
- People thrown by a blast or the Hand get up and carry on (LM01, LM02); crews of sunk ships swim ashore and walk home (mischief): not in the guide (they need the player's mischief to be under way).

## Left out: always on
Chimney smoke, sails turning, weathervanes, leaves falling and snow settling (they follow the season), water rings, gulls, pigeons, swifts, sparrows, cats, sheep, butterflies, bells, fountain jets, moored boats, the window lights, the sky.
