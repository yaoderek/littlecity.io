Little City

A tiny, hand-drawn town diorama. Something like a snow globe you build yourself.

Play

A drone flight over a town grown in the game.

Place a few blocks and they turn into a town. What each one becomes depends on where you put it. A house beside a tall tower becomes a chapel. Four blocks stacked by the sea become a lighthouse. A path becomes a street, with lamps and benches along it.

Then people move in. The miller carries flour down to the bakery, and the baker takes the bread to the stall on the square. Boats go out in the morning and come back with fish. Washing goes up on the lines. Some days there's a market. Sometimes there's a wedding.

The seasons turn. It snows. The lamps come on at dusk.

You can also skip the building: grow a whole town on a new island (a harbour town, a castle town, a farming village) and just watch it.

There are over 80 things to find. The guide has all of them.

A few questions

Who made Little City?
Derek Yao.
Is it free?
Yes. It runs in your browser: nothing to download, no account.
Does it work on a phone?
Yes, on iPhone, iPad and Android as well as on a computer.
Does my town save?
Yes, by itself, in your browser. You can also share a town as a link, or save it as a postcard.
